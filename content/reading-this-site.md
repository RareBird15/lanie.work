This site is my personal home on the internet.

I write about faith, disability, accessibility, technology, chronic illness, caregiving, games, learning, autonomy, and
the reality of being a blind, autistic, chronically ill adult trying to build a meaningful life.

Not every post is for every reader, and that's okay.

## Topics Are Labeled Clearly

I use titles, descriptions, categories, and tags to help readers decide what they want to read.

If a post is about Christianity, theology, disability, long-term care, accessibility, gaming, technology, or anything
else, I try to make that clear before the article begins.

That gives readers a choice.

You're welcome to read what interests you and skip what doesn't.

## This Site Is Not a Debate Forum

I do not host comments on this site.

That's intentional.

Some of what I write is personal, vulnerable, theological, disability-centered, or connected to real-life care needs.
I'm willing to share those things publicly, but I'm not obligated to provide an instant reaction box under every post.

If you want to contact me respectfully, you can use the contact page or reach out through one of the social links I
provide.

## Reader Agency Matters

I believe readers are responsible for their own choices.

If a post is clearly labeled as a faith reflection and you don't want to read religious content, you don't have to read
it.

If a post is clearly about chronic illness, caregiving, long-term care, or disability, and that's not something you want
to engage with, you can skip it.

If a post is about games, technology, or learning, and that's not your interest, you can move on.

Clear labeling is my responsibility.

Choosing whether to keep reading is yours.

## I Don't Censor My Whole Self Here

In a lot of spaces, I have to translate myself.

I have to make disability easier to understand, faith less complicated, illness less uncomfortable, accessibility less
urgent, or my life less complex.

This site is different.

Here, I allow more of my real context to exist in one place. I'm a Christian, a student, a writer, a disabled adult, an
accessibility advocate, a technology learner, a gamer, and a person navigating major care needs.

Those parts of my life aren't separate boxes. They overlap.

This site reflects that.

## Disagreement Is Allowed; Hostility Is Not Entitled Access

People are allowed to disagree with me.

People are allowed to dislike a post, skip a topic, or decide this site isn't for them.

What they are not entitled to is direct access to my energy, my inbox, my nervous system, or my personal space.

Respectful contact is welcome.

Hostility, bad-faith argument, mockery, harassment, or attempts to turn my personal site into a fight are not.

## Why This Site Exists

This site exists so I can have a stable place to write, document, reflect, build, and connect.

It's part portfolio, part blog, part advocacy archive, part learning log, and part home base.

Most of all, it's mine.

Thank you for reading with care.
