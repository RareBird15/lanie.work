I'm often asked about the specific tools I use to navigate a world that isn't always built for my needs. This page is a
collection of resources I trust and use regularly.

## Technology and Software

These are the primary tools that make my digital life and programming work possible.

- **[NVDA (NonVisual Desktop Access)](https://www.nvaccess.org/):** My primary screen reader. It's free, open-source,
  and highly customizable, which is essential for my development workflow.
- **[TextExpander](https://textexpander.com/):** I use this to manage repetitive typing. It reduces the physical fatigue
  of typing and the cognitive load of remembering specific strings of text or code.
- **[Ditto Clipboard Manager](https://ditto-cp.sourceforge.io/):** This allows me to track multiple copied items, which
  is crucial for my workflow and helps reduce excessive typing.
- **[VS Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/):** My main editor for Python, Lua, and SQL. It provides a stable,
  text-centric environment that works well with my screen reader.
- **[The uv Package Manager](https://github.com/astral-sh/uv):** An extremely fast Python package manager I use for
  project management and tool syncing.
- **[Foam](https://foambubble.github.io/foam/):** The tool I use inside VS Code to manage this digital garden and
  organize my thoughts.
- **[Windows Subsystem for Linux (WSL)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/wsl/):** I use this to run a Linux
  environment while still benefiting from the accessibility features of Windows.

## Services and Platforms

These platforms support my work, learning, and health management.

- **[GitHub](https://github.com/):** Essential for version control and collaboration on my code and projects.
- **[Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/):** I use this for DNS and domain management. This website is hosted on
  Cloudflare Pages, which provides a reliable and accessible platform for my content.
- **[Google Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/):** I use this for AI-assisted writing, brainstorming, and research. It
  helps me structure my thoughts when I'm feeling overwhelmed.
- **[Gmail](https://mail.google.com/):** My primary email service. I rely on its accessibility features and integration
  with other Google services.
- **[PureTalk](https://www.puretalk.com/):** A mobile service provider that offers affordable plans, which is crucial
  for staying connected without excessive costs.

## Tactile Hardware

Precision and physical feedback are crucial for my setup.

- **[Keychron K10 Max](https://www.keychron.com/):** This mechanical keyboard provides the tactile response I need to
  know exactly where my hands are. I've customized the firmware to make the layout more predictable and efficient.
- **[HydroFlask](https://www.hydroflask.com/):** Staying hydrated is a mechanical necessity for managing my health. I
  use these because they're durable and keep water at a consistent temperature, which helps with my symptoms.

## Health and Medical Nutrition

Managing multiple chronic conditions requires a systems-based approach to health.

- **[Dr. Kenny Mittelstadt](https://www.drkennymittelstadt.com/):** My functional medicine practitioner and "root cause
  detective." He works virtually with patients in Texas, California, and Florida to address the chemistry of health
  issues rather than just symptoms.
- **[FullScript](https://fullscript.com/):** The platform I use to reliably source my medical-grade supplements and
  nutrition powders.
- **MediClear Plus:** A medical food powder from Thorne I use for nutritional support when my digestive system is
  struggling.
- **UltraMeal:** A medical food powder from Metagenics designed to support metabolic health and manage nutritional
  deficiencies.

## Accessible Gaming

I recommend these games because they prioritize menus and systems over spatial navigation or timed reactions.

- **[Trimps](https://trimps.github.io/):** A deep incremental game that is entirely menu-driven and works perfectly with
  NVDA.
- **[Evolve](https://pmotschmann.github.io/Evolve/):** A text-based civilization simulation that focuses on optimization
  and automation.
- **[A Dark Room](https://adarkroom.doublespeakgames.com/):** A minimalist text-based game focused on resource
  management and exploration through menus.
- **[EmpireMUD](https://empiremud.net/):** A text game that uses coordinates and pathing, which helps me navigate
  without needing a mental map.
- **[Stellar Aeon](https://stellaraeon.com:4081/):** A text-based space exploration game that emphasizes strategy and
  resource management.
